**The Life Cycle of Ducks**

Ducks are birds that undergo a process called metamorphosis, which means they change their physical characteristics from egg to adult. This transformation is triggered by the changing environment and hormonal changes in the female duck.

During incubation (about 28 days), the eggs hatch into ducklings, which are tiny, fluffy creatures with webbed feet. The duckling stage lasts about 2-3 weeks before they start to develop feathers and grow their beaks.

**The Transformation**

As the ducklings grow, they begin to molt (shed) their old feathers in favor of new ones. This process is called "panting," where the ducks will often take slow, deep breaths to help them shed their old plumage. The molting process can last several weeks and involves a lot of energy expenditure.

**The Adult Stage**

After about 4-6 months, the ducklings have grown into adult ducks with fully formed feathers, beaks, and webbed feet. They are now ready to start breeding and laying eggs themselves!
