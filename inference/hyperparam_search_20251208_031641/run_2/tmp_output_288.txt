**Recipe: Omelette aux Truffes**

Ingredients:

* 2 eggs
* 1 tablespoon butter
* 1/4 cup sautéed truffles (wild mushrooms)
* Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions:

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk them together with a pinch of salt and pepper.
2. Heat the butter in a small skillet over medium heat.
3. Add the sautéed truffle mixture to the skillet and stir until well combined.
4. Pour the egg mixture into the skillet and cook until the eggs are set, about 1-2 minutes.
5. Fold the omelette in half and serve immediately.

**Tips:**

* Use high-quality ingredients, such as fresh mushrooms and good-quality cheese, to ensure the best flavor.
* Don't overmix the egg mixture before cooking it, or you'll end up with a tough omelette.
* If you can't find truffles, you can substitute them with other types of wild mushrooms, such as chanterelles or porcini.
