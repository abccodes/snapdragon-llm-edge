 The answer is Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson.
The famous detective agency, 221B Baker Street, was a fictional location created by Sir Arthur Conan Doyle for his stories featuring the iconic characters of Sherlock Holmes and Dr. John Watson.

However, there are several other locations that have been used as inspiration or even built as real-life versions of 221B Baker Street:

* The Sherlock Holmes Museum in London's Regent's Park
* A replica of 221B Baker Street has been constructed at the British Library in London (with permission from Conan Doyle's estate)
* In 2015, a team of filmmakers created a mock-up of 221B Baker Street using cardboard boxes and other materials.

It's worth noting that while these locations are inspired by the original story, they may not be exact replicas. The Sherlock Holmes Museum is an actual building in London where you can visit to see exhibits related to Conan Doyle's life and work, including a replica of his study at 221B Baker Street (although it's not exactly as depicted in the stories).
