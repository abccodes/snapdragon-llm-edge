Low-fat foods can be just as unhealthy as high-fat foods if they contain added sugars, refined carbohydrates, and other unhealthy ingredients. For example, low-fat yogurt with added honey may still contribute to a calorie surplus and negatively impact overall health.

High-fat foods, on the other hand, are often healthier than low-fat versions because they tend to have more fiber, vitamins, and minerals. Foods like avocado, nuts, and olive oil are rich in healthy fats that can help lower cholesterol levels and reduce inflammation.

The key is to focus on whole, unprocessed foods rather than relying on low-fat or high-fat products. Aim for a balanced diet that includes a variety of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats like nuts, seeds, avocados, and olive oil." [end of text]
