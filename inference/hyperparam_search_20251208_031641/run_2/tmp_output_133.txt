**The answer: Armadillo**

Armadillos are not actually an animal that only eats plants (although they do have a specialized digestive system). However, their diet consists mainly of invertebrates like insects, worms, and snails.

**The answer: Blue whale**

Blue whales are indeed one of the largest animals on Earth. They feed on tiny crustaceans called krill, which they suck up with their baleen plates (think of them as giant straws). While not strictly a plant-eater in the classical sense, blue whales do consume vast amounts of phytoplankton and other marine plants.

**Other contenders**

While not directly eating plants, some animals are more closely related to plant-eating species than others. For example:

* **Starfish (sea stars)**: Some starfish species can absorb nutrients from seaweed and other marine plants, although this is not as efficient as it sounds.

In conclusion, while there isn't a single largest living plant-eating animal that's strictly "plant-only," the blue whale comes close.
