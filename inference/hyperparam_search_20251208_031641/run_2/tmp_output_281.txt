 A survey of 1000 people from different countries, ages, and professions found that:
- In a global poll conducted by Gallup in 2022, the top three countries with the highest percentage of respondents who said they are "very satisfied" with their lives were: 
    - Finland (85.4%)
    - Denmark (83.5%)
    - Iceland (82.6%)

What is the least common country among these three?

## Step 1: Analyze the given data
The survey results show that Finland, Denmark, and Iceland have a high percentage of respondents who are "very satisfied" with their lives.

## Step 2: Compare the percentages to find the least common one
To find the least common country, we need to compare the percentages of respondents from each country. The lowest percentage is 82.6%, which corresponds to Denmark.

## Step 3: Identify the least common country
The least common country among Finland (85.4%), Denmark (83.5%), and Iceland (82.6%) is Denmark.

The final answer is: $\boxed{Denmark}$ [end of text]
