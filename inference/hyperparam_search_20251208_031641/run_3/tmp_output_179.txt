This riddle plays on the double meaning of the word "car" - both as an object and as a person. The answer, "the word 'car'," is cleverly used to highlight the irony in the question.

Another example:

"What words describe all car salespeople?"

Answer: "The word 'sales'."

This riddle uses the same technique, highlighting the double meaning of "sales" - both as an action and a profession. The answer is again cleverly chosen to reinforce the idea that the word "sales" describes not just the act of selling cars but also the people involved in it.

These examples demonstrate how riddles can be used to create clever wordplay, often relying on double meanings or homophones (words that sound alike). This type of wordplay is a key element of many types of puzzles and brain teasers. [end of text]
