**The Concept of Mental Disorders**

Mental disorders are conditions that affect an individual's thoughts, feelings, behaviors, or physical health. They can be caused by various factors, such as genetics, environment, trauma, or brain chemistry imbalances. The Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders (DSM-5) is a widely used guide for diagnosing mental health conditions.
