**Factors that affect diamond durability:**

1. **Impact**: Diamonds can be scratched or cracked by a hard object, such as a hammer or a rock.
2. **Chemical exposure**: Diamonds can react with acids, like vinegar or bleach, which can weaken their structure and cause them to break down over time.
3. **Physical stress**: Diamonds can crack or shatter if subjected to extreme physical forces, such as high pressure or rapid temperature changes.

**How long do diamonds last?**

While diamonds are incredibly durable, they're not indestructible. The lifespan of a diamond depends on various factors, including:

1. **Quality**: High-quality diamonds with fewer inclusions (internal imperfections) tend to be more resistant to damage.
2. **Treatment**: Diamonds that have been treated or enhanced with heat or irradiation may lose some of their durability over time.
3. **Handling**: Diamonds can become damaged if they're subjected to excessive wear and tear, such as from being dropped or handled roughly.

**Average lifespan:**

Assuming a high-quality diamond with minimal inclusions, here are some rough estimates of its lifespan:

* Average use (e.g., everyday jewelry): 10-20 years
* Moderate use (e.g., occasional wear): 5-15 years
* Heavy use (e.g., frequent exposure to harsh conditions): 2-5 years

**Tips for extending the life of your diamond:**

1. Store it properly: Keep your diamond away from chemicals, heat, and physical stress.
