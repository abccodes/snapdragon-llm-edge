1. **Contaminants from the water supply**: Tap water can contain a range of contaminants, including:

	* Parasites (e.g., Giardia, Cryptosporidium)
	* Chemicals and heavy metals

3. **Physical contaminants**: Tap water can contain physical contaminants like:
	* Sediment (e.g., particulate matter)
	* Silt
	* Sand

2. **Advanced treatment technologies**: Some cities use advanced treatment technologies like reverse osmosis (RO), ultraviolet (UV) light, or activated carbon filtration to remove contaminants from the water.

**Why are some tap waters not safe?**

1. **Old infrastructure**: Older pipes and distribution systems can be prone to contamination.
2. **Lack of maintenance**: Failure to properly maintain the treatment process can lead to inadequate removal of contaminants.
3. **Contaminant sources**: Tap water may come from a variety of sources, including:
	* Surface water (e.g., rivers, lakes)
	* Groundwater
	* Wastewater
