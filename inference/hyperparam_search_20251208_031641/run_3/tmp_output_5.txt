**Why do matadors wave their capes?**

Matadors wave their red capes as a way to distract the bull, creating an opportunity for them to attack. By waving their cape in a specific pattern, they aim to:

1. **Create a distraction**: The movement of the cape creates a visual disturbance that can divert the bull's attention away from its target.
2. **Disorient the bull**: The sudden change in direction and speed caused by the cape wave can disorient the bull, making it more likely to charge towards the matador instead of attacking them.

**The art of caparazón**

Matadors use their caparazones (red capes) as a tool for various purposes:

1. **Distracting the bull**: As mentioned earlier, waving the cape can create a distraction that allows the matador to close in on the target.
2. **Protecting themselves**: The cape also serves as a shield against the bull's attacks, protecting the matador from harm.
3. **Communicating with other animals**: In some cases, the caparazón is used to communicate with other animals, such as horses or donkeys.
