This is a reference to the Harry Potter series by J.K. Rowling, where Draco Malfoy's house is Slytherin and he lives with his family in a house called "Malfoy Manor" or simply "The Malfoys". However, in the context of this question, it seems that you are asking about who lives at number 4 Privet Drive.

Number 4 Privet Drive was actually the home of James Potter (Harry's father), Rubeus Hagrid (the gamekeeper and Care of Magical Creatures teacher), Remus Lupin (a werewolf and Defense Against the Dark Arts teacher) and Peter Pettigrew (who later revealed himself to be a wizard named Wormtail). They were all close friends. 

However, in 2010, it was announced that the house would be demolished due to safety concerns over its condition.

In 2022, it has been confirmed that number 4 Privet Drive is indeed still standing and will remain as a private residence for James Potter's descendants. [end of text]
