A cardiac arrest, on the other hand, is a sudden loss of consciousness due to a lack of oxygen and blood flow to the brain. This can occur in people with pre-existing heart conditions or those who are taking certain medications that affect the heart.

The key difference between a heart attack and a cardiac arrest is the level of damage to your body's tissues. A heart attack causes damage to only one part of the heart, while a cardiac arrest affects both sides of the heart.
Here are some additional facts about heart attacks and cardiac arrests:

*   **Heart Attack:** The most common cause of death in the United States is heart disease, which includes conditions like coronary artery disease (CAD). CAD occurs when plaque builds up inside your arteries, narrowing them and reducing blood flow to your heart. This can lead to a heart attack.
*   **Cardiac Arrest:** A cardiac arrest typically occurs due to severe chest pain or pressure that prevents the heart from pumping effectively. The most common causes of cardiac arrests include:
    *   Chest pain
    *   Shortness of breath
    *   Dizziness or fainting
    *   Confusion or loss of consciousness

In summary, a heart attack is an event where blood flow to one part of your body (your heart) is blocked. A cardiac arrest occurs when there's no oxygen and blood flow for both sides of the body.
