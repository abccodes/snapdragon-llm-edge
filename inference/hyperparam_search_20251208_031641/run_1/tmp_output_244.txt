 You might expect it to be baseball, given its historical significance and widespread popularity. However, that's not entirely accurate.

While baseball is indeed a beloved national pastime, the actual most popular sports in Japan are actually soccer (or football as it's known outside of Asia). In fact, the Japanese national team has won numerous international tournaments, including the FIFA World Cup.

Soccer is also extremely popular among young people and women. The All-Japan Women's Soccer League is one of the top professional leagues for women's soccer in Japan, with teams like Nippon FC and Kiyoshiro Yamaguchi FC competing at a high level.

In fact, soccer has become so popular that it's often referred to as "football" outside of Asia. The Japanese government has even recognized the sport as an official national pastime, which is why you'll see many schools and universities offering football training programs for students.

So there you have it – soccer is indeed the most popular sport in Japan! [end of text]
