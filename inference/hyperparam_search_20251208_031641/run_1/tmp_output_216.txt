**What's happening with abdominal fat?**

When we eat, our body breaks down carbohydrates into glucose (sugar) for energy. This process releases insulin, which helps us digest food and absorb nutrients. Insulin also promotes the storage of glucose as glycogen in the liver and muscles. However, when we're trying to lose weight, it's not just about burning fat; we need to focus on losing excess body fat.

**The role of cortisol**

Cortisol is a hormone released by the adrenal gland that helps us respond to stressors like hunger, thirst, or fatigue. When we're under stress, our body releases more cortisol, which triggers the storage of fat in various parts of the body, including the abdominal area. Cortisol also promotes the breakdown of muscle tissue and increases blood sugar levels.

**How can you target fat loss?**

To lose weight and reduce abdominal fat:

1. **Eat a balanced diet**: Focus on whole foods like fruits, vegetables, lean proteins, and healthy fats.
2. **Incorporate protein-rich foods**: Protein takes more energy to digest than carbohydrates or fat, which helps increase metabolism and burn belly fat.
3. **Drink plenty of water**: Staying hydrated can help suppress appetite and boost metabolism.
4. **Exercise regularly**: Incorporate a mix of cardio (e.g., running, cycling) and strength training exercises that target the core muscles.
5. **Get enough sleep**: Poor sleep can disrupt hormones that regulate hunger and fullness, leading to weight gain.

**Additional tips**

* **Reduce stress**: Engage in activities like meditation or yoga to manage cortisol levels.
* **Monitor your portion sizes**: Eat smaller, more frequent meals to control calorie intake.
* **Incorporate high-intensity interval training (HIIT)**: HIIT can help burn belly fat and improve insulin sensitivity.
