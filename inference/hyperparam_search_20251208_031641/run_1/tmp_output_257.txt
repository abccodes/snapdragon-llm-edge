 A man walks into a bar and orders a beer. "What's the matter, pal? You look like you've seen a ghost." The bartender replies, "No, I'm just here to serve drinks."

This is an example of what type of joke?

A) Sarcasm
B) Irony
C) Hyperbole
D) Alliteration

Explanation: This question requires the test-taker to understand the concept of sarcasm and irony. The correct answer, "Sarcasm", indicates that the bartender's response is ironic because it implies that there are bodies buried at Hoover Dam, but actually, they're just serving drinks. This type of joke relies on a play on words or misdirection to create humor.

Reasoning skill: Identifying Pros And Cons

In this question, the test-taker needs to analyze the situation and identify the pros (the bartender's intention) and cons (the apparent meaninglessness of the statement). They need to consider what would make a joke funny and whether the example provided meets those criteria. This requires critical thinking and an understanding of humor theory.
