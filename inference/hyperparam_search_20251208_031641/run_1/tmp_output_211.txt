## Step 1: Understanding the context
The question refers to a comparison between nuclear power and other forms of energy, specifically asking if there's a scientific consensus on its relative safety. This implies that we need to consider various factors related to each type of energy source.

## Step 2: Evaluating nuclear power
Nuclear power plants use atomic nuclei to generate electricity by fission (splitting). The process involves the controlled release of heat, which is then used to produce steam and drive turbines connected to generators. This method has been widely adopted for several decades but faces ongoing safety concerns due to accidents like Chernobyl.

## Step 3: Comparing with other energy sources
Other forms of energy production include fossil fuels (coal, oil, gas), renewable sources (solar, wind, hydroelectric), and nuclear power itself. Each type has its own set of risks and benefits when considering safety.

## Step 4: Assessing the scientific consensus
Scientific consensus on the relative safety of different energy types is not absolute but can be influenced by various factors such as technological advancements, regulatory frameworks, public perception, and economic considerations.

## Step 5: Considering specific examples
- Fossil fuels have been a dominant source for decades due to their abundance and relatively low upfront costs. However, concerns over climate change and air pollution raise questions about their long-term sustainability.
- Renewable energy sources like solar and wind are becoming increasingly cost-effective but still face challenges in scalability and reliability compared to traditional fossil fuel-based power plants.
- Nuclear power has faced significant safety scrutiny following accidents like Chernobyl, but it remains a viable option for baseload electricity generation.

## Step 6: Synthesizing the information
Given the complexity of evaluating each energy type's safety, a scientific consensus is not straightforward. However, there are general trends and concerns around nuclear power due to its history of accidents and ongoing debates about its long-term viability in comparison to other forms of renewable or fossil fuel-based energy.
