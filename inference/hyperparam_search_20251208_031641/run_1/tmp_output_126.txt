 This question was asked by a friend who lives in New York City, and it sparked an interesting conversation about the geography of cities.

The answer to this question is... **New Orleans**! While many people think of New York City as being located on the Atlantic coast, which makes it one of the largest cities in the United States, its location on a freshwater lake (Lake Pontchartrain) actually makes it the largest city not directly on navigable water.

So there you have it - a fascinating example of how geography can shape our understanding of what constitutes a "city"!
