 (Answer: Elmo, Big Bird, Cookie Monster, Oscar the Grouch, and Abby Cadabby)

This riddle is a play on words. The phrase "who will you see" implies that there are many people who can be seen in this street. However, the answer "Elmo, Big Bird, etc." suggests that these characters are all friends of Elmo's. This creates a clever connection between the physical location (123 Sesame Street) and the character names.

This type of riddle is called an "anagram" or more specifically, a "wordplay" riddle. It uses word association to create a humorous effect by linking two seemingly unrelated concepts together. The answer requires the solver to think creatively about how people might be friends with Elmo, making it a fun and engaging challenge for those who enjoy wordplay puzzles. [end of text]
