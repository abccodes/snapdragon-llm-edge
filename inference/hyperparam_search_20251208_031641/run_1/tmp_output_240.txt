**Low-Fat Foods:**

*   Fruits and vegetables
*   Whole grains (e.g., brown rice, quinoa)
*   Lean proteins (e.g., poultry, fish)
*   Low-fat dairy products

**High-Fat Foods:**

*   Nuts and seeds (e.g., almonds, chia seeds)
*   Avocados
*   Full-fat dairy products (e.g., cheese, whole milk)

**Key Considerations:**

1.  **Nutrient balance:** Focus on consuming a balanced diet that includes a variety of foods from all food groups.
2.  **Individual needs:** Pay attention to your personal nutritional requirements and adjust your diet accordingly.
3.  **Health benefits:** Prioritize the health benefits of each food group, rather than focusing solely on fat content.

**Tips for Healthy Eating:**

1.  **Eat a variety of foods:** Include a mix of fruits, vegetables, whole grains, lean proteins, and healthy fats in your meals.
2.  **Watch portion sizes:** Pay attention to serving sizes and control the amount of food you eat at each meal.
3.  **Stay hydrated:** Drink plenty of water throughout the day to help with digestion and overall health.
