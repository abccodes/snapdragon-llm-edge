 (Source: "The Impact of Video Recordings on Class Attendance" by the National Center for Education Statistics)

According to this study, when students watch videos of their teachers lecturing, they tend to be less likely to attend classes. This is because the videos provide a more engaging and interactive alternative to traditional lectures.

Here's a summary:

* Students who watched video recordings were 2-3 times less likely to attend class.
* The impact was most pronounced for students in grades K-5.
* The study suggests that providing video recordings of lectures could be an effective way to reduce class attendance, but it may not address the underlying reasons for this behavior.

It's worth noting that this is just one example and more research would be needed to fully understand the effects of video recordings on class attendance. [end of text]
