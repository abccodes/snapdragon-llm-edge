**Assault rifles and their classification**

An assault rifle is typically defined as a semi-automatic firearm that fires multiple rounds in rapid succession, often with a high rate of fire. Examples include the AR-15, AK-47, and others.

**US laws:**

In the US, assault rifles are classified into three categories:

1. **Fully automatic**: These firearms can fire multiple rounds without reloading (e.g., AR-15). They are heavily regulated.
2. **Semi-automatic**: These firearms require a separate trigger pull for each shot (e.g., AK-47).
3. **Assault rifle** or "assault-style firearm" (ASF): This category includes semi-automatic rifles that fire multiple rounds in rapid succession.

The US has strict regulations on assault rifles, including:

* The National Firearms Act (NFA) of 1934
* The Gun Control Act of 1968
* The Brady Handgun Violence Prevention Act of 1993
