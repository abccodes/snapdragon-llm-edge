Some factors that can influence an individual's sensitivity to spicy foods include:

*   **Hormonal fluctuations**: Hormonal changes during menstruation or menopause can affect digestive sensitivity and increase the risk of heartburn.
*   **Gastrointestinal health**: Conditions such as irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) or gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) may impact an individual's ability to tolerate spicy foods.

The relationship between spice tolerance and heartburn is complex, and more research is needed to fully understand the mechanisms involved. However, it is clear that individuals with a history of heartburn should be cautious when consuming spicy food, especially if they have underlying gastrointestinal health issues or are experiencing hormonal fluctuations.
