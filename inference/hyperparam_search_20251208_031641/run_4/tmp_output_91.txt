Spina bifida is a congenital condition that affects the development of the spine and spinal cord. It occurs when there is an incomplete closing of the neural tube during fetal development. This can lead to various complications, including:

*   **Incomplete closure**: The neural tube may not close properly, resulting in a gap between the skull and the spine.
*   **Spinal defects**: The spine may be weakened or deformed due to incomplete closure.
*   **Nerve damage**: The nerves that control movement and sensation may be damaged.

*   **Sensory loss**: They may lose sensation in their limbs, making it difficult to feel pain or discomfort.
*   **Muscle weakness**: Weak muscles can lead to difficulties with movement and balance.
*   **Respiratory problems**: In some cases, spina bifida can affect the diaphragm and other respiratory muscles.
