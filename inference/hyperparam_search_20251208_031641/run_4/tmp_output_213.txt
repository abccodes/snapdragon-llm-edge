*   **The 90% rule**: A study published in the journal "Psychological Science" found that people tend to remember about 10% of what they read.

    *   This suggests that the effectiveness of learning and retention can be greatly affected by external factors.

*   **The role of prior knowledge**: Our pre-existing knowledge plays a significant role in how much information we retain. If you already know something about a particular topic, it may not stick with you as well as if you were reading new material.
    *   This highlights the importance of understanding what's being learned and how our existing knowledge influences retention.

*   **The impact of emotions**: Emotions can significantly influence memory recall. For instance, if we're excited or motivated while reading a book, it may be more likely that information will stick with us.
    *   This underscores the significance of emotional engagement in learning processes.
