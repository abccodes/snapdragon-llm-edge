Here are some key factors that contribute to making a car stand out:

1. **Performance**: A good engine is essential for delivering impressive acceleration, handling, and responsiveness.
2. **Design and aesthetics**: A sleek, modern design can make a car look more appealing and desirable to many buyers.
3. **Interior comfort and features**: A comfortable ride, ample space, and advanced technology like infotainment systems, safety features, and convenience amenities are essential for making the driving experience enjoyable.
4. **Safety**: Advanced safety features, such as lane departure warning, blind spot detection, and automatic emergency braking, can significantly reduce the risk of accidents.
5. **Reliability and durability**: A car that lasts long without breaking down or requiring costly repairs is a significant factor in its overall value.
6. **Fuel efficiency**: Cars with good fuel economy are more environmentally friendly and cost-effective to operate.
7. **Handling and dynamics**: A responsive steering, comfortable suspension, and agile handling make for an enjoyable driving experience.

Of course, there's no one-size-fits-all answer when it comes to what makes a car great. Different people have different priorities, so the best car for someone might be something that doesn't fit into these categories perfectly.
