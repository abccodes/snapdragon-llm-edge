Neil Armstrong was an American astronaut and engineer who became the first human to walk on the Moon in 1969. He's also known for being the commander of Apollo 11 mission that successfully landed astronauts on the lunar surface. On July 20th, he famously declared "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."

Neil Armstrong passed away on August 25th, 2012.
