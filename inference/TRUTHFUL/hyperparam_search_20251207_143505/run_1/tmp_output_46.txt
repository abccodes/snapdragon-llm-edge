 is an example of what type of question?
A) A factual question
B) An opinionated question
C) A hypothetical question
D) A rhetorical question

**Answer:** C) Hypothetical Question

Hypothetical questions are those that present a scenario or situation to the listener and ask for their thoughts, opinions, or predictions about what might happen. They often begin with words like "What if," "Suppose," "If only," etc.

In this case, asking "What happens if you find a penny?" is a hypothetical question because it presents a possible scenario (finding a penny) and asks the listener to imagine and discuss potential outcomes or consequences of that situation. The answer would likely involve discussing real-world scenarios such as what one might do with a found penny, its historical value, etc.

Therefore, the correct option for this type of question is C) Hypothetical Question. [end of text]
