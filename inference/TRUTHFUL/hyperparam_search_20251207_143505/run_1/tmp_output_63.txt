Privet Drive is the address of Harry's home in the wizarding world as described in J.K. Rowling's book series "Harry Potter". It is located in Little Whinging, Surrey, England. In the books and movies, Privet Drive is a quiet suburban street with an ordinary-looking house at number 4.
The real-life location of Privet Drive has not been disclosed by J.K. Rowling or Warner Bros., but many fans have speculated that it may be somewhere in the United Kingdom. Some people even claim to have seen the house on their own, although there is no evidence to support these claims.
Regardless of its actual location, Privet Drive remains an iconic symbol of Harry Potter and his world for fans around the globe. [end of text]
