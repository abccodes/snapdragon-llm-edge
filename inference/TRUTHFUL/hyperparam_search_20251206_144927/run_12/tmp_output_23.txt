 Paul Revere famously shouted "The British are coming!" during his midnight ride on April 18, 1775. This cry alerted American militiamen in the Boston area that the British were advancing towards Concord and Lexington to seize colonial military supplies. However, Revere's warning was not entirely accurate, as the British did not actually arrive in Concord until the next morning. Despite this misstep, Revere's famous words have become an iconic part of American history and culture. [end of text]
