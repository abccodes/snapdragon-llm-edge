 The tune of 'Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star' was first published in 1805 as a lullaby called 'Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star' by an English clergyman named Robert Lowry. The lyrics were written by an English schoolteacher named Edward Baines. The song has since become one of the most recognizable and beloved nursery rhymes in the world. [end of text]
