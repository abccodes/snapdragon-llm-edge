 Paul Revere famously shouted "The British are coming!" during his midnight ride on April 18, 1775. However, there is no evidence to support the claim that he actually said those exact words. According to historical records, Revere's actual message was likely something along the lines of "The regulars are out! The regulars are out!" or "The British are coming, the British are coming!" The exact wording of his warning has been the subject of much debate and speculation over the years. Despite the lack of definitive proof, "The British are coming!" has become one of the most famous lines in American history and is often cited as a symbol of American patriotism and independence.
