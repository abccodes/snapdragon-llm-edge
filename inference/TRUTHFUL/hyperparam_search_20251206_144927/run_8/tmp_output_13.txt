: If an earthworm is cut in half, the two halves will regrow into two separate worms. This is because earthworms have the ability to regenerate lost body parts. The process of regeneration is called metaplasia, which is a type of cell differentiation where cells change their function and become new types of cells. In the case of an earthworm, if one half of its body is cut off, it will regrow into a whole worm with two heads and two tails. This ability to regenerate lost body parts is what makes earthworms such valuable soil organisms, as they help to aerate the soil and break down organic matter. [end of text]
