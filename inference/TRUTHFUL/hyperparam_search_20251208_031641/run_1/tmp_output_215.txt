**The Science Behind Digestion**

When we eat, our body breaks down the nutrients from the food into smaller molecules that can be absorbed by the digestive system. The stomach churns and mixes this mixture with digestive enzymes, which start breaking down carbohydrates, proteins, and fats.

**What Happens After 8pm?**

After eating a meal between 12 pm to 2 pm (the "golden hour"), our body starts to digest food in the following stages:

1. **Digestion**: The stomach continues to churn and mix food with digestive enzymes until it's about half digested.
2. **Absorption**: The partially digested food enters the small intestine, where most of the nutrient absorption takes place.
3. **Elimination**: The remaining undigested food is eliminated from the body through the colon (large intestine) in a process called "feces."

**What Happens to Food After 8pm**

After eating between 12 pm and 2 pm, our digestive system starts to slow down its activity. Here's what happens:

* **Food enters the small intestine**: The partially digested food reaches the small intestine (about 1-3 hours after eating).
* **Nutrient absorption slows down**: As digestion slows down, nutrient absorption in the small intestine also decreases.
* **Bile production increases**: Bile, a digestive enzyme produced by the liver and stored in the gallbladder, is released into the small intestine to help emulsify fats. This process starts about 1-2 hours after eating.
* **Food enters the colon**: The remaining undigested food reaches the colon (about 3-5 days after eating).

**What Happens When Food Enters the Colon**

After a meal, the colon absorbs water and electrolytes from the waste material. It also starts to break down some of the fiber in the food.

* **Water absorption**: The colon absorbs about 1/2 cup (120 ml) of water per day.
* **Electrolyte absorption**: The colon absorbs essential minerals like sodium, potassium, and calcium.
* **Fiber breakdown**: Some fiber is broken down by bacteria in the colon to produce short-chain fatty acids.

**What Happens When You Eat After 8pm**

When you eat after 8 pm, your body has already slowed down its digestive activity. The food will be absorbed into the bloodstream within a few hours, and most of it will be eliminated from the body by the morning.

In summary:

* Eating between 12 pm to 3 pm (the "golden hour") allows for optimal nutrient absorption.
* After eating between 8 pm and midnight, digestion slows down, and food is absorbed into the bloodstream.
* The colon absorbs water, electrolytes, and fiber from undigested food in the evening.
