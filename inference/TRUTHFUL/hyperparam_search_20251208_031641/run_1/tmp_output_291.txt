## Step 1: Understanding Luminous Resonance
Luminous resonance is a concept in physics where an object's luminescence can be influenced by the presence of another object or material with different properties, such as temperature, composition, or even shape. When you rub an old lamp, it might seem like just a simple action, but what actually happens is that the energy from your skin and the surrounding environment interacts with the lamp.

## Step 2: Exploring the Physics Behind Luminous Resonance
The key to understanding luminous resonance lies in the concept of thermal radiation. When you rub an object, such as the old lamp, you transfer heat energy to it. This heat can cause the material's temperature to rise slightly. As a result, the emitted light from the lamp becomes more intense and its color shifts towards longer wavelengths.

## Step 3: Applying Luminous Resonance to the Lamp
