1. **In-N-Out Burger (California)**: Known for their simple menu, high-quality ingredients, and iconic Double-Double.
2. **Shake Shack (New York City)**: A modern day "roadside" burger stand that has gained a cult following with its Angus beef burgers and crispy crinkle-cut fries.
3. **Five Guys (Washington D.C.)**: This chain is famous for their abundance of toppings, hand-formed patties, and endless bun options.
4. **Smashburger (Multiple locations)**: A unique cooking method where the burger patty is smashed onto the grill to create a crispy crust and juicy interior.
5. **Umami Burger (Los Angeles)**: This gourmet burger joint offers creative flavor combinations like the "Bacon Jam" and "Truffle Mac 'n Cheese."
6. **Patty & Bun (Chicago)**: A modern take on traditional burgers with creative toppings, sauces, and a focus on quality ingredients.
7. **The Counter (Multiple locations)**: A fast-casual burger chain that offers high-quality burgers made with fresh, never-frozen beef.
8. **Five Guys' sister restaurant, BurgerFi (Boston)**: Known for their hand-formed patties and creative toppings like grilled onions and mushrooms.

Of course, there are many more excellent burger joints out there, but these are some of the most well-known and beloved in the country.
