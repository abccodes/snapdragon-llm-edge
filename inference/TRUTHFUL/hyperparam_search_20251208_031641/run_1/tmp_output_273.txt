## Step 1: Understanding Sleep Stages
Sleepers enter different stages of sleep, each lasting around 90-120 minutes. These stages include non-rapid eye movement (NREM) and rapid eye movement (REM). NREM is further divided into three stages, with the first being light sleep or transition to REM.

## Step 2: Transitioning from Sleep
When someone wakes up suddenly, they might not immediately remember what happened while asleep. This can lead to confusion about their current state of consciousness.

## Step 3: Re-entering Normal Consciousness
The brain takes time to adjust to being awake after a period in sleep or REM sleep. It's essential for the body and mind to gradually transition back into normal functioning, which might involve some disorientation due to the sudden change from deep sleep to wakefulness.

## Step 4: Potential Effects on Behavior
Being suddenly awakened can cause confusion, disorientation, and even affect one's mood or behavior temporarily. The brain is still adjusting its processes after a period of inactivity.
