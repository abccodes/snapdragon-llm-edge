"That's one small step for a man on a mission to walk and one giant leap for Mankind."

However, some people have questioned this interpretation, suggesting that the phrase might be more complex than initially thought.

In an interview with CNN in 2012, Neil Armstrong's daughter, Judith Armstrong, revealed that her father had actually said:

"That's one small step for mankind, to reach out and become a part of something much bigger."
