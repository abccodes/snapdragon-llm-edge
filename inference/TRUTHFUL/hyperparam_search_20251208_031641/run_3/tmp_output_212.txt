Auditory learners prefer listening to audio recordings or lectures. They may benefit from podcasts, audiobooks, or online courses with accompanying video lessons. Auditory learners tend to learn better when they can listen to the material being presented.

Kinesthetic learners prefer hands-on activities and experiments. They often use manipulatives like blocks, puzzles, or games to practice concepts. Kinesthetic learners tend to learn faster in a more interactive environment.
