**Handling Baby Birds:**

1. **Wash your hands**: Before touching a baby bird, wash your hands thoroughly with soap and water.
2. **Support their body**: Hold the bird securely but carefully support its body, making sure not to squeeze or crush it.
3. **Be gentle**: Move slowly and gently around the bird's neck, wings, and tail to avoid causing stress or injury.

**Handling Baby Birds Safely:**

1. **Keep them away from predators**: If you're handling a baby bird in a predator-prone area (e.g., near a nest), keep it at a safe distance.
2. **Avoid sudden movements**: Sudden movements can startle the bird, causing it to fly or become aggressive.
3. **Don't handle too many birds**: Handle only one or two birds at a time, and make sure they're not stressed or injured.

**What to Do If You Touch a Baby Bird:**

1. **Contact local wildlife rehabilitation centers**: Reach out to local wildlife rehabilitation centers for guidance on how to care for the bird.
2. **Provide food and water**: Offer the bird food (e.g., sunflower seeds, fruits) and water in a safe location, such as a designated feeding area or a birdbath with fresh water.
3. **Monitor its condition**: Keep an eye on the bird's behavior, appetite, and overall health to ensure it receives proper care.
