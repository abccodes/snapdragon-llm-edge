1. **Ionization**: As the plane flies through the air, its exhaust contains ions and free electrons. These particles interact with the surrounding air molecules, causing them to break down into smaller fragments.
2. **Condensation**: The condensed water vapor from these particles forms tiny droplets in the atmosphere. This process is known as condensation.
3. **Trail formation**: As more and more droplets are formed, they accumulate on the surface of the plane, creating a trail or streak that can be seen from space.

* **Altitude**: The amount of water vapor in the air affects the size and density of the particles.
* **Temperature**: Higher temperatures lead to more efficient ionization and condensation.
