Gandhi's philosophy is based on several key principles:

1. **Non-violence**: Gandhi believed that violence only leads to more violence, creating a cycle of retaliation. Instead, he advocated for peaceful means to achieve social change.
2. **Civil Disobedience**: Gandhi used non-violent protests and civil disobedience to challenge unjust laws and policies. This approach allowed him to reach large numbers of people without resorting to violence.
3. **Passive Resistance**: Gandhi believed that individuals could make a positive impact by simply being present, observing, and learning from others. Passive resistance involved not actively resisting the system but rather allowing it to passively influence society through its actions.
4. **Empathy and Understanding**: Gandhi emphasized the importance of understanding and empathy in building bridges between different groups. He believed that individuals should strive to see things from other people's perspectives.
