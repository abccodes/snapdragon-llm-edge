This is a classic example of the "false friend" phenomenon in language, where two words that are similar but have different meanings can be used interchangeably. In this case:

* "Barack" and "Baracka" (the name) are homographs.
* "Obama" refers to the person's last name.

This is a common issue in languages with many loanwords from other languages, where words may not always convey their intended meaning or have different meanings depending on the context. [end of text]
