3. **Lactic acid buildup**: As you continue exercising, lactic acid builds up in your muscles. This is a natural byproduct of anaerobic metabolism (when oxygen is not available). Lactic acid can lead to muscle cramps, fatigue, and decreased performance.
4. **Muscle damage leads to inflammation**: When you exercise intensely or for an extended period, you cause micro-tears in your muscle fibers. 5. **Muscle protein synthesis is impaired**: After intense exercise, your body's natural repair process prioritizes rebuilding and repairing damaged tissues over producing new muscle tissue.
