While there is no definitive answer, let's dive into some possible scenarios:

2. **Extreme weather events**: Climate change is already linked to more frequent and intense heatwaves, droughts, and storms. These events can have devastating effects on human populations, infrastructure, and ecosystems.
3. **Water scarcity**: Changes in precipitation patterns and increased evaporation due to warmer temperatures could lead to water shortages, affecting agriculture, industry, and human consumption.
4. **Food insecurity**: Climate change affects crop yields, leading to food shortages and price increases. This can have severe consequences for global health, economies, and social stability.

1. **International cooperation**: Global agreements like the Paris Agreement provide a framework for countries to work together on climate action.
