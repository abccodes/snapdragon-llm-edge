**The context**

In the story, Draco Malfoy asks Harry Potter about his parents' names at a Quidditch match. **The significance**

However, in J.K. Rowling's original manuscript and interviews, it was actually a deliberate choice to leave the answer ambiguous. In an interview, Rowling explained that she wanted readers to fill in the blank with their own imagination:

"I didn't want to give away too much of the story at once... I wanted people to think about who they were going to be when they got to Hogwarts."

**The impact**

This line has become a beloved meme and cultural reference point, symbolizing the power of storytelling and the human desire for mystery. It's also been used in various contexts, from fan fiction to advertising.
