As they boarded the ships, Adams and Otis were met with resistance from the British sailors. However, instead of fighting back or trying to escape, Adams reportedly said: "If we can't have our tea, let's drink it in the streets!" This quote has become a famous phrase associated with the Boston Tea Party.
