**The Basics**

In astrology, the zodiac signs are divided into 12 categories: Aries to Pisces. Each sign represents a specific period of time and has its own unique characteristics, traits, and energies associated with it.

**Saturn**

Saturn is often referred to as the "King of the Zodiac" because of its association with authority, discipline, and responsibility. People born under Saturn are known for their practicality, hard work ethic, and strong sense of duty.

Here are some key characteristics that people born under Saturn:

* **Responsibility**: You're likely to be very responsible and reliable in your personal and professional life.
* **Discipline**: You have a strong will and can maintain focus on your goals, even when faced with challenges or setbacks.
* **Practicality**: You value hard work, efficiency, and effectiveness in all aspects of life.

**The Implications**

If you're born under Saturn (e.g., January 1st to December 21st), here are some potential implications:

* **You may feel a strong sense of responsibility towards others**, which can sometimes manifest as a need for control or authority.

**The Key Takeaway**

People born under Saturn are often seen as dependable, responsible, and practical individuals. While they may face challenges related to their need for control and authority, these traits can also serve them well in achieving success and stability.
