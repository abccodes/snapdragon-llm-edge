 Japan is the only country that has consistently shown an interest in and appreciation for anime. It's not just limited to fans of Japanese culture, but also includes people from all over the world who have been exposed to it.

From its early days as a niche market, anime has grown into a global phenomenon with millions of viewers worldwide. The popularity of anime is evident in various aspects such as:

*   **Anime conventions**: These events bring together fans and creators alike, offering an immersive experience that showcases the latest trends and releases.
*   **Merchandise industry**: Anime-inspired merchandise like figurines, collectibles, and apparel has become a significant part of popular culture worldwide.
*   **Influence on film and television**: Anime has had a profound impact on the global entertainment landscape, inspiring numerous adaptations in various forms.

While anime is not universally loved by everyone, its unique blend of storytelling, artistry, and cultural significance has made it a beloved phenomenon across the globe. Its influence extends beyond traditional media to shape popular culture as a whole. [end of text]
