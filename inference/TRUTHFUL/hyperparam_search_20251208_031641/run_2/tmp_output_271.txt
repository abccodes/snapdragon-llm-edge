**Breast Cancer: The Leading Cause of Death Among Women**

* Breast cancer is a leading cause of death among women in the United States.
* According to data from the National Cancer Institute (NCI), breast cancer was responsible for an estimated 40,000 deaths in the U.S. in 2020 alone.

**Lung Cancer: The Second Leading Cause of Death Among Women**

* Lung cancer is a leading cause of mortality among women worldwide.
* According to the NCI, lung cancer was responsible for approximately 22,400 deaths in the United States in 2020.

**Other Important Statistics**

* In addition to breast and lung cancers, other important statistics include:
	+ Colon cancer: The second most common type of cancer among women, accounting for an estimated 38,000 deaths per year.
	+ Pancreatic cancer: A rare but aggressive form of cancer that affects approximately 14,000 women in the United States each year.

**Reducing Cancer Risk**

* Early detection and screening are critical to reducing the risk of developing breast and other cancers.
* The American Cancer Society recommends:
	1. Regular mammograms for women aged 40-49
	2. Annual clinical breast exams for women over 20 years old
	3. Breast self-exams every month, with a focus on the top two areas (breast tissue)
	4. Avoiding exposure to tobacco smoke and secondhand smoke

By staying informed about cancer risks and taking proactive steps to reduce them, we can work together to prevent these devastating diseases from affecting our loved ones.
