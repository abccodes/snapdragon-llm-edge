**Ancient Mythologies**

In many ancient cultures, the concept of a single "road" that leads nowhere was seen as a paradoxical idea. For example:

* In Greek mythology, the hero Hercules (Heracles in Roman) embarked on a journey to complete his twelve labors, but ultimately found himself lost and confused.
* The Norse god Odin had many paths to follow, each leading him to different realms of existence.

These mythological stories suggest that the idea of a single "road" is not only impractical but also potentially limiting. Instead, they highlight the importance of multiple pathways and choices in navigating life's journey.
