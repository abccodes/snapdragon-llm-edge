## Step 2: Evaluate the impact on class attendance
If video recordings of lectures are made available to students outside of regular class hours, it could potentially reduce their motivation to attend classes in person. This is because they might feel that watching videos at home allows them to learn more independently and at a faster pace than attending live sessions.
