 The question was asked by a man who had been watching the funeral procession of Walt Disney, and he wanted to know where his body would be buried. This is an example of what type of humor?

Answer: Black Humor

Explanation: Black humor typically involves making light of serious or taboo subjects in a darkly comedic way. In this case, the man was asking about Walt Disney's funeral procession, which is a somber and dignified event. By poking fun at it with his question, he was using black humor to make a point.

Note: The other options are not correct because:

* White Humor typically involves making light of something in a more innocuous way.
* Red Humor usually involves making jokes about death or the afterlife.
* Green Humor is often associated with absurdity and illogic, but it's not directly related to humor types. [end of text]
