 

I have a friend who has been flying for years and I asked him about this, but he just shook his head and said "you're not listening to me."  So I decided to do some research on my own.  After several hours of searching, I finally found out that it is actually the smoke from aircraft leaving behind their exhaust trails.

So, you see, when an airplane flies through the air at high speeds, its engines produce a lot of heat and gases that are expelled into the atmosphere as exhaust. This process creates a trail or "smoke" in the sky that can be seen for miles.  I'm glad my friend didn't think me completely crazy! [end of text]
