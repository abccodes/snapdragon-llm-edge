Neil Armstrong's famous phrase "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind" is often misquoted as simply "one small step." However, it was actually part of his more comprehensive statement:

"I have walked on the moon. I am here today to dedicate this mission and all that we've accomplished so far."

Armstrong's actual words were: "That's one small step... for a man on the Moon, but I think that might be a giant leap for mankind." This phrase was part of his speech when he landed on the lunar surface on July 20, 1969. [end of text]
