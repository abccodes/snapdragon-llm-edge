 Neil Armstrong famously said "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind" when he stepped off the lunar module Eagle onto the surface of the Moon on July 20, 1969. The quote has become one of the most famous in history and is often referenced in popular culture. However, there are some variations of the exact words that Armstrong said. According to NASA's official transcript of the Apollo 11 mission, Armstrong said "That's one small step for a man, one giant leap for mankind." The phrase "a man" was added later by NASA to make it sound more formal and appropriate for a historic moment. However, some sources report that Armstrong actually said "That's one small step for a person, one giant leap for mankind." This variation is less common and may have been influenced by the fact that Armstrong was speaking to Mission Control in Houston, Texas, which was primarily staffed by women at the time. Regardless of the exact words, Armstrong's quote has become an iconic symbol of human achievement and the spirit of exploration. [end of text]
