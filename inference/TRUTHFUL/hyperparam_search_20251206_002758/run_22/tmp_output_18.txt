Gum is made up of a mixture of ingredients including sugar, flavorings, and a base material called elastomer. When swallowed, the elastomer in gum can take several hours to break down in the digestive system. During this time, it may cause discomfort or blockages in the intestines.
In rare cases, swallowing large amounts of gum can lead to serious complications such as perforation of the intestines or obstruction of the airway. However, these are relatively uncommon and most people who swallow gum will not experience any problems.
If you do swallow gum and experience symptoms such as abdominal pain, bloating, or difficulty passing gas, it is generally safe to continue eating and drinking. If your symptoms persist or worsen, it is a good idea to seek medical attention.
