 - This question is about the safety of monosodium glutamate (MSG), a common flavor enhancer found in many foods. The answer is that MSG has not been scientifically proven to be harmful to humans in small doses.

This classification is based on extensive safety assessments and is widely accepted by regulatory agencies around the world.

In conclusion, while there may be some anecdotal evidence or individual reports of adverse effects associated with MSG consumption, there is no scientific proof that MSG is harmful to humans in small doses.
