


: Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is known as the "Planet of Limits" due to its slow rotation and cold temperatures. In astrology, Saturn is associated with discipline, responsibility, and structure. People born under Saturn are often seen as hardworking, disciplined, and practical. They may also be perfectionists and have a strong sense of duty and responsibility. However, they can also be critical and perfectionistic, which can lead to stress and anxiety. Overall, people born under Saturn are known for their reliability, stability, and ability to handle challenges with resilience. [end of text]
