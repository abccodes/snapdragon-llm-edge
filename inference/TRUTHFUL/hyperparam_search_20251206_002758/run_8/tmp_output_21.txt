

 "The Queen says to her mirror, 'Who is the fairest of them all?' "
This quote comes from the 1937 animated film Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs. It is one of the most famous lines in the movie, and it has become a cultural icon. The line is spoken by the Queen, who is jealous of Snow White's beauty and wants to find out who is the fairest of them all. [end of text]
