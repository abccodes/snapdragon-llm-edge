 Neil Armstrong famously said "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind" when he stepped off the lunar module Eagle onto the Moon's surface on July 20, 1969. The quote has become one of the most recognizable in history and is often reproduced in books, movies, and other media.
However, it's worth noting that Armstrong didn't actually say those exact words. He had practiced his speech extensively and was prepared to say something along those lines if the opportunity arose. But when he stepped onto the Moon, he was so focused on the moment and the significance of what he was doing that he ended up saying a slightly different version of his prepared statement.
