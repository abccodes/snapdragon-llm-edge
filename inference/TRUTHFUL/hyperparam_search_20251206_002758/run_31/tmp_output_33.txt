 Area 51 is a United States Air Force facility located in Nevada, primarily used for testing experimental aircraft and weapons systems. The facility was established in 1958 as part of Project Mogul, which aimed to detect Soviet nuclear tests through high-altitude balloons. Over the years, Area 51 has become synonymous with top-secret military projects and extraterrestrial research, although its actual purpose is not widely known due to strict secrecy measures. The facility is managed by the Air Force Research Laboratory and is located on a remote area of the Nevada Test and Training Range. Area 51 is one of the most closely guarded facilities in the United States, with only a select few individuals having access to its restricted areas. Despite its reputation for top-secret projects, Area 51 has been involved in various publicized events over the years, including the development of stealth aircraft and missile defense systems.
