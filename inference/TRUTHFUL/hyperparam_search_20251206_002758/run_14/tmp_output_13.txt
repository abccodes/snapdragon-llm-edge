Earthworms are segmented worms that live in soil and play a crucial role in breaking down organic matter and aerating the soil. They have a simple body structure with a head, tail, and a series of rings or segments along their length. Each segment has a pair of muscles that help the worm move forward by contracting and relaxing.

When an earthworm is cut in half, it can survive for a short period of time. The head of the worm will continue to function, but the body will not be able to move or digest food. The segments below the cut will eventually die due to lack of nutrients and oxygen. However, if the cut is made near the head, the worm may be able to regenerate its body segments.
