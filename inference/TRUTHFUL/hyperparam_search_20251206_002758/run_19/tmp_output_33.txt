 Area 51 is a United States Air Force facility located in Nevada, primarily used for experimental aircraft and technologies. The facility was established in 1958 as part of Project Mogul, which aimed to detect Soviet nuclear tests through high-altitude balloons. Over time, the focus shifted to developing advanced military technology, including stealth aircraft and missile systems.
Area 51 is known for its secrecy and has been the subject of numerous conspiracy theories and rumors. However, the facility's primary purpose remains classified. The Air Force maintains that Area 51 is used for testing and development of new technologies, with a focus on national security and defense. The facility is not open to the public and access is restricted to authorized personnel.
