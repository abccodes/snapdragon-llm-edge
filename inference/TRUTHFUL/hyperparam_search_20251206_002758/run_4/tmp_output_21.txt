

 "The exact words that the Queen says to her mirror in the 1937 film Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs are: 'Mirror, mirror, on the wall, who is the fairest of them all?'"
This line of dialogue is one of the most famous and iconic from the film, and it has become a cultural reference point. The Queen's question to her mirror serves as a metaphor for her desire to be the most beautiful and desirable person in the kingdom, and it sets the tone for the rest of the story.
The line was written by the film's screenwriter, Joseph L. Mankiewicz, and it has been performed by many different actresses over the years, including Shirley Temple, Judy Garland, and Elizabeth Taylor. In the original 1937 version of the film, the Queen is played by Margaret Hamilton, who delivered the line with a distinctive Scottish accent. [end of text]
