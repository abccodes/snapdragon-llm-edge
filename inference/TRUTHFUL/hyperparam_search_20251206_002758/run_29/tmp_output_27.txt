 Neil Armstrong famously said "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind" when he stepped off the lunar module Eagle onto the surface of the Moon on July 20, 1969. The quote has become one of the most recognizable in history and is often recited as a symbol of human achievement and progress. However, it's worth noting that Armstrong didn't actually say those exact words. According to NASA, he said "That's one small step for man; one giant leap for mankind" during his moonwalk on July 20, 1969. The words "for man" were added later by NASA officials to make the quote more grammatically correct and easier to understand. Despite this, the quote has remained one of the most famous and memorable in history. [end of text]
