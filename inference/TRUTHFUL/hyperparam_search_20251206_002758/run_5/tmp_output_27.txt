 Neil Armstrong famously said "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind" when he stepped off the lunar module Eagle onto the surface of the Moon on July 20, 1969. The quote has become one of the most famous in history and is often referenced in popular culture. However, there are some variations of the exact words that Armstrong said. According to NASA's official transcript of the Apollo 11 mission, Armstrong said "That's one small step for a man, one giant leap for mankind." The transcript also includes a brief pause before Armstrong finished speaking, which was not included in many of the early recordings of the moon landing. In addition, some versions of the quote include the word "for" instead of "a" before "man," as in "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind." Despite these variations, the original quote remains one of the most famous and recognizable in history. [end of text]
