

MSG is a flavor enhancer commonly used in food. It has been widely used in China for many years and has not caused any significant health problems. However, some people still believe that MSG can cause harm to the human body. In fact, there have been many studies on MSG, but most of them do not show that MSG is harmful to humans in small doses. The World Health Organization (WHO) also states that MSG is generally safe for consumption. Therefore, it can be concluded that MSG has not been scientifically proven to be harmful to humans in small doses.
