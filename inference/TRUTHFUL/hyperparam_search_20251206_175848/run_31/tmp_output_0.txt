Watermelon seeds are not toxic, but they can cause digestive issues in some individuals.
Some common symptoms of eating too many watermelon seeds include:
- Abdominal pain
- Diarrhea
- Nausea
However, it's important to note that these symptoms are relatively rare and usually only occur when someone eats a large number of seeds at once. Most people can enjoy watermelons without any issues.
