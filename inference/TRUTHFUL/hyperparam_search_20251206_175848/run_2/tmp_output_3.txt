But what if we were to take this knowledge one step further? What if we could create a new type of chili pepper that is even spicier than any other variety currently available?
This is where genetic engineering comes into play. By manipulating the DNA of a chili pepper plant, scientists can introduce genes from other plants or organisms that contain higher levels of capsaicin.
One example of this is Capsicum chinense var. frutescens, commonly known as the habanero chili pepper. This variety has been found to have significantly higher levels of capsaicin than many other chili peppers, making it one of the spiciest varieties available.
By introducing genes from the habanero into a different type of chili pepper plant, scientists could potentially create a new variety that is even more spicy than the habanero. This would be achieved by increasing the levels of capsaicin in the seeds and membranes, as well as other parts of the plant.
