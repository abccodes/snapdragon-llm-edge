 

Answer: July 4, 1776

But wait! There's more!

Did you know that on July 3rd and July 5th of 1776, the Continental Congress voted to approve the Declaration of Independence? However, it wasn't until August 2nd (not July 4th) that the document was officially adopted.

So, while we celebrate July 4th as Independence Day in the United States, there's a bit more history behind it! [end of text]
