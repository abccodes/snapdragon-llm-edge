The answer is "the forbidden fruit." The forbidden fruit was not a specific type of fruit. Instead, it referred to any kind of fruit that God had forbidden them from eating.

In Genesis 3:6, God says, "Now we may eat the fruit of the trees, but we must not eat from them; we are forbidden to eat from every tree in the garden except the tree with the knowledge of good and evil." This is often translated as "the forbidden fruit."

So, Adam and Eve ate any kind of fruit they found in the Garden. They didn't have a specific type of fruit in mind when they took it.

It's worth noting that many scholars believe that the story of Adam and Eve eating the forbidden fruit was originally intended to be a metaphor for human experience rather than a literal account. The story may have been meant to illustrate the consequences of disobedience, or to convey God's judgment on humanity's rebellion against Him. [end of text]
