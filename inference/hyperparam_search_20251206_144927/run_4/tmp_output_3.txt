 This question is often asked by people who enjoy spicy foods. The answer may surprise some, as it's not necessarily the hottest part of the chili pepper that packs the most heat.
The spiciest part of a chili pepper is typically the seeds and membranes inside the fruit. These parts contain capsaicin, the compound responsible for the heat in chili peppers. While the flesh of the chili pepper may be less spicy, it can still contribute to the overall flavor of the dish.
Some people prefer to remove the seeds and membranes before eating or cooking with chili peppers, as they can be a bit more difficult to digest and may cause some people to experience heartburn or other digestive issues. However, for those who enjoy the heat, leaving these parts in can add an extra kick to their dishes. [end of text]
