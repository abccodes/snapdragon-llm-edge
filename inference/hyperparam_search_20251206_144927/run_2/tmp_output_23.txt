 Paul Revere famously shouted "The British are coming!" during his midnight ride on April 18, 1775. However, there is no evidence to support the claim that he actually said those exact words. According to historical records, Revere's actual message was likely something along the lines of "The regulars are out! The regulars are out!" or "The British are coming! The British are coming!" Revere's warning played a crucial role in alerting American militia and helping them prepare for the battles that would follow. [end of text]
