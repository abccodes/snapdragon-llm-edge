 Neil Armstrong famously said "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind" when he stepped off the lunar module Eagle onto the surface of the Moon on July 20, 1969. The quote has become one of the most recognizable in history and is often repeated in discussions about space exploration and achievement. However, it's worth noting that Armstrong didn't actually say those exact words. According to NASA, he said "That's one small step for a man, one giant leap for mankind." The difference is subtle, but it's an important distinction to keep in mind when discussing the famous quote. Despite the slight variation in wording, the meaning and impact of the quote remain the same. It has become a symbol of human achievement and exploration, and will likely be remembered for generations to come. [end of text]
