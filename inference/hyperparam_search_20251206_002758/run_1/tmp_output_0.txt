Watermelon seeds are rich in essential nutrients such as magnesium, phosphorus, zinc, copper, and selenium. They also contain dietary fiber, vitamins C and E, and antioxidants like lycopene and beta-carotene. These components contribute to their health benefits.

**1. Nutritional Value:**

Watermelon seeds are a good source of protein, healthy fats, and various minerals. They provide around 3 grams of protein per ounce (28 grams), which is about 6% of the daily recommended intake for adults. Additionally, they contain essential fatty acids like linoleic acid, which can help reduce inflammation.

**2. Fiber Content:**

The seeds are also high in fiber, providing approximately 1 gram per ounce (28 grams). Fiber helps with digestion and can contribute to weight management by making you feel full longer.

**3. Antioxidant Properties:**

Watermelon seeds contain antioxidants such as lycopene and beta-carotene. These compounds help protect cells from damage caused by free radicals, which are unstable molecules that can lead to chronic diseases.
