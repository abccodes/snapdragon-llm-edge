Gum is made up of a mixture of natural and artificial ingredients, including sugar, flavorings, and preservatives. When swallowed, the body breaks down the gum into smaller pieces, which can then pass through the digestive system without causing any problems.
However, swallowing large amounts of gum at once can cause blockages in the digestive tract. This is because the gum can form a hard mass that is difficult for the body to break down. In rare cases, this can lead to serious complications such as perforation of the intestines or obstruction of the airway.
