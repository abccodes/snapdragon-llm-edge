The spiciness of a chili pepper is determined by a compound called capsaicin, which is found in the seeds and membranes of the fruit. The seeds and membranes contain the highest concentration of capsaicin, so they are generally considered to be the spiciest part of the chili pepper.
However, it's important to note that the spiciness of a chili pepper can also depend on how it is prepared. For example, if you remove the seeds and membranes from a chili pepper before cooking or eating it, the remaining flesh may not be as spicy as the whole fruit.
In general, the following parts of a chili pepper are considered to be the spiciest:
- Seeds: The seeds contain the highest concentration of capsaicin and are generally considered to be the spiciest part of the chili pepper.
- Membranes: The membranes surrounding the seeds also contain capsaicin and may be slightly less spicy than the seeds
