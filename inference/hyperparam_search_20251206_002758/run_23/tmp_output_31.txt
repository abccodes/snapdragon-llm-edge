1. **Mission Overview**: The Apollo 11 mission was the first and only time humans walked on the lunar surface. It was launched by NASA on July 16, 1969.

2. **Key Astronauts**: Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, and Michael Collins were the astronauts aboard the spacecraft. Armstrong and Aldrin made history by becoming the first humans to set foot on the moon on July 20, 1969.

3. **Landing Site**: The Apollo 11 mission landed in a region called the Sea of Tranquility, near the lunar mare Tranquillity.

4. **Apollo 11's Journey**:
   - **Launch**: The spacecraft lifted off from Kennedy Space Center in Florida.
   - **Moon Orbit**: After a three-day journey, the Apollo 11 spacecraft entered lunar orbit.
   - **Descent to Moon**: On July 20, Armstrong and Aldrin descended to the moon in the Lunar Module Eagle.
