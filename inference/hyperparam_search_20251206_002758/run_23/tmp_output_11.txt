 - The sun appears white when viewed from space. This is because the sun emits a broad spectrum of light, including all colors of the visible spectrum. When this light reaches an observer on Earth, it is scattered and absorbed by the atmosphere, which can give the appearance of different colors depending on the time of day and weather conditions. However, when observed directly from space, the sun appears as a bright white object due to its uniform emission of all colors of light. [end of text]
