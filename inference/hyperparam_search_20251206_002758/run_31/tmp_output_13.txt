An earthworm is a segmented worm that lives in soil and helps to aerate the soil and break down organic matter. Earthworms are important for maintaining healthy soil and supporting plant growth.
If an earthworm is cut in half, the two halves will not survive. The head of the worm contains the brain and digestive organs, which are necessary for survival. If the head is removed, the body will continue to grow, but it will not be able to function properly without its head.
The tail of the worm also contains important organs that are necessary for survival. If the tail is cut off, the worm will be able to survive for a short period of time, but it will eventually die due to lack of nutrients and oxygen.
