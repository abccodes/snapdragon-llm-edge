 is a common question. The answer is yes, humans did indeed land on the moon during the Apollo 11 mission in 1969. Here are some key points about this historic event:

- **Mission Overview**: Apollo 11 was NASA's first crewed mission to land on the Moon. It was launched on July 16, 1969, from Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

- **Key Players**: The mission was commanded by Neil Armstrong, who was the first person to set foot on the lunar surface. Michael Collins piloted the command module, while Buzz Aldrin joined Armstrong on the lunar module Eagle.

- **Landing and Exploration**:
  - On July 20, 1969, Armstrong and Aldrin descended to the lunar surface in the Eagle.
  - They spent about two and a half hours exploring the Moon's surface, collecting samples, and conducting experiments.
  - Armstrong famously said, "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."

- **Return Journey**:
  - After completing their mission, Armstrong, Aldrin, and Collins returned to Earth on July 24, 1969.
