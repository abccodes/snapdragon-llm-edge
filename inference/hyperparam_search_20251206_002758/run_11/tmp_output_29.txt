Assistant The quote "Insanity is doing the same thing over and over again and expecting different results" is often attributed to various sources, including:

- **Albert Einstein** (though there's no direct evidence he said this)
- **Thomas Edison** (this is a common misattribution)
- **Benjamin Franklin**
- **Winston Churchill**

However, the most widely accepted attribution for this quote is **Benjamin Franklin**. 

Franklin wrote in his autobiography, "I have always found that people's minds are like mirrors; they reflect back to you whatever you throw at them." This aligns closely with the idea of expecting different results from the same actions.

Therefore, the correct answer is:

C) Benjamin Franklin [end of text]
