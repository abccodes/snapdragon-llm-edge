But according to NASA's official records, Armstrong did indeed use those exact words on the moon. The quote was recorded on a tape and played back during a press conference on July 20, 1969, shortly after Armstrong stepped onto the lunar surface.
The quote has since become an iconic symbol of human achievement and exploration, and is often referenced in discussions about space exploration, technology, and the future of humanity.
