The correct answer is C. Both A and B.

Explanation: When an aircraft flies through the atmosphere, it leaves behind a trail of both exhaust gases and water vapor. Additionally, the heat generated by the aircraft can cause water vapor to condense into small droplets, forming a visible trail behind the plane.
