If you go outdoors in cold weather with wet hair, you may feel very cold and uncomfortable. The water in your hair can freeze and become brittle, which can cause it to break off easily. Additionally, the cold air can make your skin feel dry and itchy. To avoid these problems, it's best to let your hair dry completely before going outside in cold weather. You can use a hair dryer on a low setting or leave it out to air dry. If you have long hair, you may want to wear a hat or scarf to keep your head warm. [end of text]
