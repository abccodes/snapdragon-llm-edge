Here are some of the potential consequences of swallowing gum:
1. Obstruction: If you swallow a large piece of gum, it can become lodged in your throat or esophagus and block the passage of food or liquid. This can be extremely dangerous and require immediate medical attention.
2. Digestive issues: Swallowing gum can cause digestive problems such as bloating, gas, and constipation. This is because the body cannot break down the plastic-like substance in gum, which can lead to digestive discomfort.
3. Tooth damage: If you swallow a piece of gum that is still intact, it can scratch or damage your teeth over time. This is because the hard surface of the gum can cause microscopic scratches on the enamel of your teeth.
4. Allergic reaction: Some people may be allergic to gum or other ingredients in gum, which can cause an allergic reaction if swallowed. Symptoms of an allergic reaction can include hives, swelling, and difficulty breathing.
