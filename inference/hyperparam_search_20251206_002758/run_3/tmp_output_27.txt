 Neil Armstrong famously said "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind" when he stepped off the lunar module Eagle onto the surface of the Moon on July 20, 1969. The quote has become one of the most recognizable in history and is often recited as a symbol of human achievement and progress. However, it's worth noting that Armstrong didn't actually say those exact words. According to NASA, he said "That's one small step for a man, one giant leap for mankind." The difference may seem minor, but it highlights the importance of precision in language. Armstrong's words were carefully chosen to convey the significance of his achievement and to inspire future generations of explorers and dreamers. The quote has since become an enduring symbol of human progress and the spirit of adventure that drives us to explore new frontiers. [end of text]
