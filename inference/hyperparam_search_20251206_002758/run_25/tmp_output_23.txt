 Paul Revere famously shouted "The British are coming!" during his midnight ride to warn the American militia of the approaching British forces in 1775. However, it's worth noting that Revere didn't actually say those exact words. The exact quote he used was "The regulars are out!" which was a reference to the British Army's use of the term "regulars" for their troops. Despite this, the phrase "The British are coming!" has become synonymous with Revere's famous ride and is often cited as one of the most iconic moments in American history. [end of text]
