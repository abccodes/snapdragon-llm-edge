


: Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and is known as the "Planet of Limits" due to its slow rotation and cold temperatures. In astrology, Saturn is associated with discipline, responsibility, structure, and limitations. People born under Saturn are often seen as hardworking, disciplined, and responsible individuals who value stability and order in their lives.

Saturn is also associated with challenges and obstacles, as well as the need for perseverance and resilience. People born under Saturn may face more difficulties and setbacks than those born under other signs, but they are also more likely to develop a strong sense of purpose and determination.

Overall, being born under Saturn can be seen as a gift, as it instills in individuals a strong work ethic, discipline, and sense of responsibility. However, it can also be challenging at times, as people may need to overcome obstacles and persevere through difficult situations. [end of text]
