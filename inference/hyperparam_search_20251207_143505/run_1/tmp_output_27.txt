On July 20th of that year, he stepped out onto the lunar surface and said one of the most memorable phrases in history: "That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."
However, it is worth noting that Armstrong did not actually say those exact words on the Moon. In fact, they were written by NASA officials before the mission began.
The actual words Armstrong spoke on the lunar surface were slightly different. He said: "Houston, Tranquility Base here. The Eagle has landed." This was followed by a brief pause before he continued with his famous quote about taking one small step for man and one giant leap for mankind.
