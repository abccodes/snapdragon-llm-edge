**Step 1: Understanding the Question**
The question "What is the spiciest part of chili pepper?" is asking about the source of the heat or spiciness in chili peppers. This requires knowledge about how chili peppers are grown, what makes them spicy, and where the heat is concentrated within the plant.

**Step 2: Exploring the Science Behind Spiciness**
Chili peppers contain a compound called capsaicin, which is responsible for their spicy taste. Capsaicin is found in the white pith (the inner membrane) of the chili pepper, as well as in the seeds and membranes surrounding them.

**Step 3: Engaging in an Interactive Experiment**
To demonstrate where the spiciness is concentrated, the parent can conduct a simple experiment with their child. They can use a spoon to scrape some of the white pith from a chili pepper and put it on the tongue. The child should notice that this part is much hotter than other parts of the chili pepper.
